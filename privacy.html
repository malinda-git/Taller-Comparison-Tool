Privacy policy

Last updated: 10/10/2026.

What the chart stores

The chart runs in your browser. Items you create and your chosen unit are kept in your browser's local storage on your own device so they are there next time. This information is not sent to our servers.

Images you add

Images you choose with the custom image option are read by your browser to draw the chart. They are not uploaded to our servers.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

If this site shows ads, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can manage personalised advertising in your Google ad settings. If this site uses analytics, it collects anonymous usage information such as pages visited and device type.
