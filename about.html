About HeightLine

HeightLine is a free tool for comparing the heights of people, buildings and everyday objects on one shared scale. It was built to make size differences easy to see, whether you are comparing two family members or a person against the tallest building in the world.

Building facts such as location, completion year, architect, main contractor and client are gathered from public sources. Items marked as basic details are not fully verified. If you find a mistake, please tell us through the contact page and we will correct it.

Heights of public figures are approximate values as commonly reported and are included for fun and comparison only.
