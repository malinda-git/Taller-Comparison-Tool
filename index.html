    How to use the height comparison chart

    Pick people, celebrities, buildings or everyday objects from the list and they appear side by side on one shared scale. Type a new height and the figure grows or shrinks straight away. Switch between centimetres, metres and feet and inches at any time.

    Measuring the difference between two heights

    Press Compare, then click two figures on the chart. A bracket appears between their heights and shows the gap, for example 150 cm against 160 cm gives a 10 cm difference. The panel under the chart repeats the gap in both metric and imperial units.

    Why do small figures look tiny next to tall buildings?

    Everything is drawn to the same scale. A building of 828 m is roughly 470 times taller than a person of 1.75 m, so the person becomes a thin mark at the bottom. Use Zoom to move closer to the ground, then scroll up to follow the building to its top.

    How accurate are the heights?

    Heights for celebrities are approximate values as commonly reported. Building details were compiled from public sources, and entries marked as basic details have not been fully checked. Always confirm important figures against an official source.
